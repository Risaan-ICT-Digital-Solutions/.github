## 👋 Welcome to Risaan ICT!

Welcome to the official GitHub home of **Risaan ICT & Digital Solutions**. We are passionate about practical ICT skills, creative problem-solving, software, and the power of technology to help our communities grow.

Here, we share our projects, document what we learn, and collaborate with people interested in digital innovation.

## 💻 What We Focus On

- 🌐 **Web development** — websites and modern web applications
- 🧩 **Software solutions** — useful tools for real-world challenges
- 🎓 **ICT learning** — tutorials, practice projects, and digital skills
- 🤝 **Open-source collaboration** — sharing code and learning together
- 🚀 **Digital innovation** — exploring new ideas and technologies

## 🎯 Our Mission

To make technology more useful and accessible by encouraging ICT education, teamwork, and practical digital solutions.

## 🌍 Our Vision

A future where more people in Somalia and beyond can learn, create, and succeed through technology.

## 🚀 Explore & Contribute

1. **Browse our repositories** to discover projects and learning resources.
2. **Read each project's README** for setup instructions and goals.
3. **Open an issue** to ask a question, suggest an improvement, or report a bug.
4. **Contribute** through discussions and pull requests when a repository welcomes contributions.

👉 **[View all our repositories](https://github.com/orgs/Risaan-ICT-Digital-Solutions/repositories)**

## 🤝 Be Part of the Community

Whether you are an ICT student, developer, educator, or technology enthusiast, you're welcome to explore our public work, learn with us, and share helpful ideas.
